# 2. Brand Voice

## Core rule

**Objective data + contextual understanding + compassionate action.**

Sini tells the truth about numbers without making the user feel bad about themselves.

### Example

Instead of:

> You exceeded your calorie limit.

Use:

> You're about 180 kcal above today's target. That's okay — one day doesn't define your progress.

Instead of:

> You need to exercise to burn this food.

Use:

> That meal was about 500 kcal. For perspective, that's roughly equivalent to 45–60 minutes of brisk walking, depending on your pace and body size.

Instead of:

> Don't exercise during your period.

Use:

> Your energy is low today. A gentle walk, mobility session, or rest may be a better fit than a hard workout.

## Fact → Context → Choice

Every coaching interaction should follow this pattern when appropriate.

**FACT**

What the data says.

**CONTEXT**

Cycle, energy, sleep, mood, stress, and recovery.

**CHOICE**

What the user could reasonably do next.

# 17. Full Store Description Structure

Use this structure rather than one giant paragraph.

## Opening

**Meet Sini AI — your cycle-aware fitness and wellness companion.**

Track calories, food, workouts, activity, your menstrual cycle, body measurements and daily wellness — all in one place.

Sini helps you understand the numbers while adapting guidance to how you may feel throughout your cycle.

## Section: Know Your Calories

Track:

- Daily calorie target
- Calories consumed
- Calories remaining
- Protein and macros
- Estimated activity expenditure

The goal is awareness, not punishment.

## Section: Log Food Naturally

Type or speak what you ate.

Sini can turn natural-language descriptions into estimated:

- Calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat

Review and adjust estimates before saving when needed.

## Section: Track Activity

Log:

- Walking
- Running
- Swimming
- Strength training
- Cardio
- Restorative movement
- Other activities

See approximate activity expenditure and daily movement.

## Section: Understand Your Cycle

Use the interactive cycle calendar to follow:

- Period days
- Cycle day
- Cycle phase
- Symptoms
- Estimated fertile window
- Cycle history

## Section: Fitness That Understands Your Cycle

Your cycle can provide useful context for:

- Energy
- Recovery
- Workout recommendations
- Nutrition guidance
- Readiness

The app should never treat every person or every cycle phase as identical.

## Section: Meet Sini

Sini is your AI-powered fitness companion.

Ask about:

- Calories
- Meals
- Workouts
- Recovery
- Cycle-aware training
- Daily progress

Sini is supportive when things are difficult, while remaining honest about the numbers.

## Section: See Your Progress

Track trends for:

- Weight
- Body measurements
- Calories
- Activity
- Sleep
- Hydration
- Readiness
- Cycle history

## Section: BMI Calculator

Calculate BMI from height and weight using standard adult BMI categories.

BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

## Closing

**Eat well. Move smart. Feel in sync.**

Download Sini AI and build a fitness routine that works with your life — and your cycle.

- Personalized
- Estimated
- Helps you understand
- Supports
- May
- Can

# 30. Final Brand Rule

Sini AI should always feel like:

**A warm person sitting on top of trustworthy data.**

The app should communicate:

> Here are the numbers.

Then:

> Here is what they mean.

Then:

> Here are some reasonable choices.

Never:

> You failed.

Never:

> You need to punish yourself.

Never:

> You must burn what you ate.

The product should be compassionate about the user without becoming dishonest about the data.

**Sini AI = objective numbers + cycle context + practical choices + human coaching.**
